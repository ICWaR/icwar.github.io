The OSH vision

Why One Sustainable Health?

Health challenges are interconnected and require solutions beyond traditional healthcare. OSH brings human, animal, plant and environmental health together through science, technology, policy and innovation to help create resilient ecosystems for future generations.

  • Responding to climate change, antimicrobial resistance (AMR) and emerging health threats.
  • Using AI, digital innovation and data-driven approaches.
  • Connecting science, policy, industry and communities.
