Navigating the demands of academic life and rigorous research can sometimes feel overwhelming, and we want to remind you that you never have to shoulder it alone.

The ICWaR Wellness Committee is dedicated to ensuring that your time at the Centre is safe, balanced, and fully supported. We are writing to encourage you to reach out to us regarding any queries, concerns, or suggestions you might have for improving mental health and overall well-being within our department.

Whether you are facing academic stress, personal challenges, or simply have an idea to make our community better, our door is always open. Please feel free to connect with any of our committee members via email, phone, or by visiting us directly at the Centre.

We firmly believe that everyone deserves to work and study in an environment where their personal growth and mental health are actively cared for.
